# Buckshot Peps

## What it is
Two surfaces in one app:
- **Store** (`/`): public online store for research compounds (peptides in vials). Register: **brand**.
- **Admin** (`/admin`): the Peps CRM. Orders, shipping, inventory, money split between two partners. Register: **product**.

## Who uses it
- Store: adult (21+) buyers of laboratory research compounds, mostly on a phone, comparing vendors on trust: is the batch tested, can I see the COA, is checkout clear.
- Admin: the two owners, non-technical, usually on a phone between other things. Every label must be plain language.

## Brand voice
Clinical, exact, unhurried. A lab supplier's printed catalog, not a supplement ad. States facts (lot, purity, test date) and never makes claims.

## Hard rules
- No dosing, health, protocol, benefit or results claims anywhere. Research use only line in the footer of every page.
- Never expose cost, profit, margins or stock counts publicly.
- Red and white. Deep red (default `#9f1d22`) used sparingly: buttons, links, badges, key moments. White and off-white sections, near-black text, thin neutral borders.
- One sans family (Geist), tight headings, generous body line height.
- Avoid: gradient glows, glassmorphism, neon, purple, emoji, generic AI-startup layouts, icons everywhere, fake stats, filler copy.
- Subtle motion only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Must work at 390px and on desktop.

## Design reference
arcticdose.com for structure and polish only (never its text, images, labels, branding or code).

## Anti-references
Supplement-store hype, bodybuilding forum aesthetics, crypto-neon, SaaS hero-metric templates.
